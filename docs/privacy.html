Bookie: Read Later

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Bookie does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything you save stays on your device.

What Bookie stores

To work, Bookie keeps the following on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • the web addresses (URLs) and titles of the pages you save,
  • the collections you create and which pages belong to them,
  • your appearance setting (system, light or dark).

This information never leaves your device through Bookie. There is no account, no server and no sync. You can delete individual pages in the app, delete everything under Settings → Delete all data, or remove it all by deleting the app.

Like other app data, it may be included in your device backups (for example iCloud Backup). Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by Bookie.

Sharing a page to Bookie

When you choose Bookie in the Share menu of another app, the link, its title and any text that app shares are passed to Bookie on your device so you can save them. Bookie only receives what you explicitly share.

Opening saved pages

Saved pages open in an in-app browser provided by iOS. The website you open may collect data under its own privacy policy. Bookie does not see or record what you do on those websites.

No tracking, ads or analytics

Bookie contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. It does not use your data to track you across apps or websites.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give the developer anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You can turn this off in your device's privacy settings.

Children

Bookie does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
