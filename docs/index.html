Common questions

How do I save a page from Safari?

Open the page, tap Share and choose Bookie. If you don't see it, scroll the row of apps to the end, tap More and add Bookie. The link and title are filled in for you; pick a collection and tap Save.

How do I move or delete a saved page?

Long-press the page. You can change its title or collection, or delete it.

Are my pages synced or backed up?

Bookie has no account or sync. Pages are stored only on your device, and are included in your regular device backups. Deleting the app deletes your saved pages.

Does Bookie save the page for offline reading?

No. Bookie saves the link and title; the page loads from the web when you open it.
